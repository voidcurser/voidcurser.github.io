Privacy Policy for Despesas

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Despesas is a personal finance application designed to help users manage expenses, income, budgets, savings, investments, recurring transactions, and financial goals.

1. Financial Data

Financial information entered into Despesas is stored locally on the user's device by default.

When a user explicitly requests an AI Financial Review, selected financial information required to generate that review is securely transmitted to our backend service and to our AI service provider for processing.

This information may include financial amounts, transaction categories, income, expenses, budgets, savings, investments, recurring commitments, financial goals, and historical financial trends.

The AI Financial Review does not require a Despesas user account, and the financial information submitted for the review is not intentionally associated with the user's name, email address, or other direct account identity.

2. AI Processing

Despesas uses OpenAI technology to generate optional AI Financial Reviews. Only information necessary to provide the requested review is submitted for processing.

AI-generated information is provided for informational purposes and should not be considered professional financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

3. Purchases and Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are processed through Google Play. Despesas may communicate with our backend and Google Play services to verify subscription status and determine access to Premium features.

Payment card and billing information is handled by Google Play and is not collected or stored by Despesas.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal or financial information.

Data is shared with service providers only when necessary to operate specific features requested by the user, such as AI Financial Review processing and Google Play subscription verification.

5. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information transmitted between the application, our backend services, and third-party service providers.

6. Data Retention

Financial data stored locally remains on the user's device until it is modified or deleted by the user or the application is removed.

AI Financial Reviews may be stored locally on the user's device so that previous reviews can be displayed and used to improve the continuity of future reviews.

7. Children's Privacy

Despesas is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Despesas evolves. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
