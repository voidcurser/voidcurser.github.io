Effective date: November 14, 2026

Cashin'Up, also identified as Despesas in some app and service listings, is a personal finance application for recording income, expenses, budgets, savings, investments, recurring transactions and financial goals. This policy explains what is kept on your device, which optional services involve outside providers, and how advertising privacy choices work.

1. Financial records and local storage

Core financial records are stored locally in the app's device storage, including its WebView storage and native preferences where used. You do not need a Cashin'Up account or bank connection to record finances. The app does not currently offer automatic Cashin'Up cloud backup or cross-device financial synchronization. Its Android configuration disables app backup; exported files that you save or share separately remain outside the app's control.

Records may include amounts, dates, categories, financial goals, labels, notes, recurring commitments, asset values and settings. Exported JSON backups are readable files and can remain wherever you save or share them, even after deleting app data.

2. Optional AI financial reviews

When you choose to generate or update an AI Financial Review, Cashin'Up sends a structured financial profile through its backend hosted by Cloudflare to OpenAI for processing. This can include monthly financial summaries and history, category and income-source names, budgets, goals, recurring-payment descriptions or notes, net-worth totals and up to three earlier reviews for continuity. User-entered labels or descriptions may contain personal information; the summaries should not be treated as anonymous. Processing these financial details and user-written content lets the service generate a review tailored to your financial situation. You can keep using local financial recording without requesting an AI review.

Generated reviews are returned to the app and can be saved locally. Simply recording expenses or viewing an existing saved review does not trigger a new AI review. Backend, hosting-provider and AI-provider operational data and retention are separate from your local records and are subject to the relevant providers' practices. AI suggestions are informational, not professional financial, tax, legal or investment advice.

Online financial reviews use the current server-verified authentication described below. The app obtains or renews authorization when needed for a requested review; an existing local Premium indication alone does not authorize an online review. The backend also records minimal operation metadata to enforce usage limits and prevent duplicate work. Those records do not contain the full financial profile or generated review text.

The AI request disables storage of the generated response as retrievable API state using OpenAI's store:false setting. This does not establish zero retention or memory-only processing: provider abuse-monitoring, operational logs and other applicable retention can still apply. See OpenAI's API data controls.

All three optional OpenAI data-sharing controls are disabled for the API account used for this service. We have not opted into optional sharing of API content for model improvement through those controls. API call logging is configured as enabled per call, and the backend explicitly sends store:false on review requests. This disables retrievable response-state storage for those calls; it does not disable all provider safety, operational or cache retention. These settings do not constitute a special Zero Data Retention agreement.

3. Google Play subscriptions

Google Play handles Premium subscription checkout and payment-card details. Cashin'Up uses Google Play Billing and its backend to verify purchases, acknowledgements and subscription status. Purchase tokens are processed for verification, and the backend stores related purchase-token hashes and subscription-status metadata, including product and plan identifiers, acknowledgement and verification status, relevant dates and linked-purchase references. These records support subscription functionality, purchase verification and prevention of fraudulent access. These hashes are not necessarily anonymous. Cashin'Up does not request or store your payment-card number through its purchase flow.

The app keeps locally recognized Google Play Premium status across restarts and temporary verification or network failures. A short local cache deadline alone does not remove that recognition; a confirmed entitlement result can update it. This local state is separate from short-lived online authorization and does not replace Google's subscription or payment records.

Lifetime Premium and existing authorized access

If you redeem a Lifetime Premium code, Cashin'Up sends the code, a device-generated public key and a signed proof to its backend. The backend records a hash of the code, a grant reference, the public-key fingerprint and creation, redemption or revocation metadata. The private key remains in Android Keystore and is not sent to the backend. The app stores the local credential and grant state needed to recognize the device's access.

Recognized Lifetime access persists locally and is separate from expiring online API sessions. Online reviews still require valid server authorization. Clearing app storage, uninstalling the app or changing devices can remove the local key or credential needed for access; this is not financial-data backup or a promise of automatic transfer to another device. Existing authorized developer or reviewer access, where present, also uses its applicable grant or proof information rather than creating a Cashin'Up user account.

Online authentication and security processing (V2)

Depending on the source of Premium access, online authentication uses a locally generated installation identifier, request and challenge identifiers, request digests, entitlement or grant references, and temporary credentials or signed proofs. Where the Google Play Integrity flow applies, Cashin'Up sends a temporary Integrity token to its Cloudflare backend for verification with Google. Purchase verification can also process the Play purchase token. Registered public-key or credential-version information is used for the applicable device-bound proof flows.

The backend evaluates the relevant app, licensing, device, request and entitlement checks and stores challenge, session, grant, replay-prevention, usage-limit and review-operation metadata. These checks protect online functionality, verify access and prevent fraud, replayed requests, abusive usage and duplicate AI work. It does not store user private keys in these tables. Some identifiers and digests can relate to a device, entitlement or request and should not be treated as anonymous. Security/session expiry is not the same as expiry or deletion of permanent Premium ownership records.

Google's advertising software collects and shares relevant identifiers, approximate-location information derived from IP addresses, app/ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Advertising personalization depends on applicable permissions and choices. Google and participating advertising vendors may receive this information, subject to the relevant configuration, region and choices. Cashin'Up's own advertising integration does not send your financial records or raw payment-notification text to AdMob.

When available, the message lets you accept, decline or manage advertising-related purposes and vendors. Legitimate-interest controls allow choices about the processing presented on that basis; the default setting is not itself consent or proof that every processing purpose is lawful. Google's separate Consent Mode setting is off; UMP privacy messaging remains in use.

Programmatic limited ads are disabled. This turns off Google's optional programmatic limited-ad mode, which Google describes as using invalid-traffic-only cookies or local storage on traffic without advertising consent. Other advertising-related storage and processing may still occur where permitted, including SDK or ad-creative caching, privacy-choice storage, IP-address processing for delivery, and applicable diagnostics or security processing. Declining consent or disabling this mode does not mean that all advertising-related storage, network requests or processing stops. For details of Google's practices, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

5. Optional Android notification access

When a Premium user enables Payment Detection and grants Android's special Notification Access, Cashin'Up can read notifications from supported payment apps to identify possible outgoing payments. Android grants broader notification access than the app's supported-source filter uses. The app's detector parses supported notifications on-device; it does not upload or retain their raw text. Pending suggestions can contain a merchant, amount, currency, timestamp and deduplication identifiers. A suggestion becomes a financial expense only if you confirm it.

Pending suggestions are limited to 25, with seven-day expiry enforced when the store is accessed or updated; this is not a guarantee of deletion at an exact scheduled time. Payment Detection can be switched off in Cashin'Up, and Notification Access can be revoked in Android settings. Local reminder notifications are a separate feature.

7. Retention, export and deletion

Financial records and saved reviews generally remain in local app storage until you change, reset or remove them. To reset financial information, open Settings → Data → Delete all data, type the confirmation word displayed in the app and confirm. This resets the main financial state and settings; it also attempts to disable Payment Detection and clear pending suggestions. The in-app data-reset function resets financial information but is not a universal deletion of every identifier, purchase record, consent setting or server/provider record. Clearing Cashin'Up storage in Android settings or uninstalling removes app storage more broadly and can remove device-bound Premium credentials. Delete exported backups and copies you saved or shared separately. Revoke Notification Access separately in Android settings if you enabled it. Resetting data, requesting deletion, clearing app storage or uninstalling does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions separately in Google Play. Local deletion does not undo information already processed by external services.

Google and other providers retain advertising, purchase and operational information under their applicable policies and service arrangements. Subscription-verification records and some backend operational data are retained separately from the device; Cashin'Up does not currently offer a one-tap deletion of every remote record. For requests concerning data handled by Cashin'Up, follow the Cashin'Up Data Deletion page or email voidcurser.dev@gmail.com, preferably with the subject Cashin'Up Data Deletion Request. Requests can cover identifiable purchase-verification, access/security and AI-operation records under our control, as well as support correspondence. We review what can be deleted and explain any applicable retention exception. You can manage Google advertising choices through the mechanisms described in section 4.

Existing storage and expiry behavior

  • Local financial data and saved reviews: remain on the device until changed, reset, cleared or removed. Exported files must be deleted separately. The financial reset does not clear every separate local identifier, entitlement or consent record.
  • Pending payment suggestions: capped at 25, with seven-day expiry applied when the suggestion store is accessed or updated.
  • Local access and operation records: recognised Premium state, device-bound credentials and the installation identifier can persist independently of a financial reset. Online session tokens are held in memory. A pending-review operation identifier/digest may remain across restart until the outcome is resolved; it does not contain the full financial profile or review text.
  • Temporary backend security records: challenges are valid for up to two minutes; sessions for up to 30 minutes, subject to earlier authority expiry; reviewer grants for up to seven days, subject to the campaign end date. The replay/request ledger expires after 24 hours, while quota and operation-lock records use their configured windows. The configured cleanup task runs every 15 minutes and deletes the covered expired records. These are validity and scheduled-cleanup settings, not a guarantee of physical erasure at the exact expiry time or proof that every scheduled run succeeded.
  • Durable backend metadata: subscription-verification records, Lifetime/developer grants and audit history, AI-operation metadata and subscription-notification delivery metadata have no general automatic deletion schedule in the current implementation. Where subscription notifications are enabled, their ledger stores message identifiers, digests and delivery/processing metadata, rather than notification bodies or raw purchase tokens.
  • Hosting logs and recovery history: the backend uses Cloudflare Workers Free, with logs enabled and no Logpush subscription. Cloudflare's currently published Workers Free log-retention window is three days. D1 Time Travel retains seven days of recovery history for this account. These windows are separate from active database rows and the manual recovery copies described next. Removing an active row does not instantly remove recovery-history copies. See Workers Logs and D1 limits.
  • Separate database recovery copies: protected local database exports and restore-verification copies are retained for controlled deployment and recovery. They contain the backend purchase/access, security and operation metadata present at the time of the snapshot. They are separate from users' financial JSON exports and from D1 Time Travel, so the seven-day Time Travel window is not their deletion deadline. No general automatic deletion schedule for these copies was found. They are included in the manual retention review below, and are retained only while a specific recovery, unresolved incident/dispute or applicable legal purpose remains. Copies no longer needed are removed through that process; erasures must be reconciled before a recovery copy is restored to active service.
  • AI provider processing: the request uses store:false, but provider safety logs and prompt-cache storage may still apply. OpenAI's published standard API guidance allows abuse-monitoring retention, normally up to 30 days with stated exceptions, and describes separate cache behavior. Those are provider terms, not a Cashin'Up promise that all copies disappear after 30 days. No special Zero Data Retention arrangement is assumed.

Retention and deletion criteria

We assess whether durable records are still necessary when handling a deletion request, ending/revoking access, resolving an uncertain AI operation, and during regular retention reviews. Purchase/access metadata is needed while it supports an active entitlement, restoration or an unresolved purchase issue. AI-operation metadata is needed while usage accounting, duplicate-request handling or an uncertain outcome still requires it. Revocation/audit and notification-delivery metadata is kept only while a documented security, replay-prevention or dispute purpose remains. Support correspondence is needed while the inquiry or rights request is open and, afterwards, only to the extent needed to document its handling or address an actual dispute. We remove identifiable records when no such purpose or applicable legal obligation remains; we do not retain every record for the longest possible legal period simply because it exists. We explain any concrete reason for retaining data in response to a deletion request. The current implementation requires eligible remote deletions to be handled manually.

We monitor the contact mailbox for privacy requests and review durable records at least monthly, as well as at the events described above. Any separately retained log exports, manual backups and support copies under our control are included in this purpose review. Recovery copies are handled to avoid restoring data that has been erased from active systems. We normally respond to GDPR rights requests within one month; where a lawful extension applies, we communicate it and the reasons as required. This response period is not a promise that all provider recovery copies are erased within one month.

Removing entitlement or access records can affect the ability to use or restore Premium. Deleting Cashin'Up records does not itself erase Google Play's purchase records, advertising records or every provider log/backup. Where we use a processor and a request concerns that processing, we address the request with the relevant provider where required. Independent providers may also retain records for their own lawful purposes. We do not promise immediate deletion of every external copy. Using online features again can create new records.

Cashin'Up uses device access controls and technical protections for certain native features and communications with services. Connections used for the app's backend and AI processing use HTTPS.
