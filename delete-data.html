Cashin'Up Data Deletion

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Cashin'Up (also identified as Despesas) has no user accounts. Your core financial records are stored on your device. This page explains how to delete local data and request deletion of data handled by Cashin'Up's backend.

Request deletion by email

Email voidcurser.dev@gmail.com with the subject Cashin'Up Data Deletion Request.

  1. Tell us which data you want deleted, or say that you want deletion of all data we can associate with your use of Cashin'Up.
  2. Mention whether you used a Google Play subscription, a Premium access code, or AI Financial Reviews. You do not need to send your financial records.
  3. We may reply to request the minimum additional information needed to locate the relevant records and verify that the request relates to you. An email address alone may not identify device or purchase records because Cashin'Up has no account system.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, private keys, purchase tokens, access codes, or a complete financial export in your initial email.

We will review the request and explain what can be deleted and whether any records must be retained. Emailing us does not remotely erase data on your device.

Delete financial data on your device

  1. Open Cashin'Up and go to Settings → Data → Delete all data.
  2. Read the confirmation, type the confirmation word shown in the app (DELETE in English or APAGAR in Portuguese), and confirm.

This resets local financial records, including transactions, categories, budgets, goals, recurring records, savings, investments, net-worth history and saved AI reviews, together with the app's main settings. The reset also attempts to turn off Payment Detection and clear pending payment suggestions.

Limitations: the financial reset does not erase every local identifier, Premium entitlement or credential, advertising-consent setting, or backend/provider record. To remove app storage more broadly, use Android's app settings to clear Cashin'Up's storage/data, or uninstall it. Android labels may vary. Clearing storage or uninstalling can remove device-bound Premium credentials.

Delete exported JSON backups and copies you saved or shared separately. These files are outside the app's reset. If you enabled Android Notification Access, you can revoke it separately in Android settings. Local deletion cannot undo data already sent for an AI review or other online processing.

Backend data covered by a deletion request

Depending on the features you used, a request can cover records held by Cashin'Up such as:

  • Purchase-verification records: purchase-token hashes and subscription-status, acknowledgement and related verification metadata.
  • Access and security records: installation identifiers, authentication challenges and sessions, Premium grant references, public-key fingerprints, and related access or abuse-prevention metadata.
  • AI review operation records: request identifiers, digests, timestamps, usage and completion status. These backend records contain operation metadata rather than the full financial profile or generated review text.
  • Support correspondence: information you supplied when contacting us.

We can delete associated records under our control where we can identify them and they are no longer needed for the purposes below. Removing access or entitlement records may affect your ability to use or restore Premium features. There is no in-app button that deletes every remote record.

What may be retained

Some records may need to be retained where necessary to verify purchases or access rights, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce usage limits, avoid duplicate AI operations, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal obligations. We will explain any applicable retention exception when responding to your request.

Some temporary security records expire and are cleaned up periodically. Expiry does not guarantee immediate physical deletion. Subscription, Premium grant and AI operation records do not all have a general automatic deletion schedule. We do not promise a fixed retention period for these records.

Deleting local data or Cashin'Up backend records does not automatically delete Google Play's purchase records, advertising data, information already processed by OpenAI or Cloudflare, or copies in provider logs or backups. Those records are subject to the relevant provider's practices and applicable requirements. Where relevant, we will explain provider-related limitations; we cannot promise immediate deletion of every provider or backup copy.

Google Play subscriptions are separate

Deleting data, requesting deletion, clearing app storage or uninstalling Cashin'Up does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel it separately in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or open Google Play subscription management.
